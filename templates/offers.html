Frequently Asked Questions

Does the discount reduce the theatre's original ticket price?

No. The original theatre ticket price is 100% remitted to the cinema exhibitor. The discount applies strictly to Ticket Mania's convenience fee.

Is the discount applied once per ticket or once per booking?

The discount is applied once per entire booking (₹5 for odd total tickets, ₹10 for even total tickets).

What happens if I cancel a simulated booking?

Under our prototype demo rules, 100% of the theatre ticket price is refunded, the booking is marked cancelled, and the seats are instantly released back to the hall.
